Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • The expenses you record, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep you signed in.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to run Spendly: to create your account, store your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share it with third parties except where required by law.

4. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

5. Your choices

  • You can view, edit or delete your expenses at any time.
  • You can update your profile information from your account.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and its data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Return to Spendly and get in touch with us. See also our Terms & Conditions.
